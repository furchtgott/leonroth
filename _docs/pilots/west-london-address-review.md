## Editorial choices and review

Address delivered on September 30, 1956, transcribed from the 2006 reprint. The original numbered divisions jump from 7 to 9; the gap is retained. Two reprint editorial notes are labelled separately after the address. Source wording, including apparent printer errors, is retained.

The scan supplies European Judaism 39(1), Spring 2006, pp. 16–26. Its closing note says the address originally appeared in The Synagogue Review 31(3), November 1956. This edition uses the scanned reprint year, without changing the existing 1956 bibliography entry. Recheck source wording such as “first and principle reason”, “He who have”, “Judaism in not”, “Mainmonides”, and “vanished and are more” before acceptance; do not silently correct it.
