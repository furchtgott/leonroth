## Editorial choices and review

Only Roth's introduction, headed מבוא, is transcribed. The separate הקדמה is by H. H. Ben-Sasson and is excluded. The introduction's date is Elul 5709; the book's title page says 5710 and the bibliography gives 1950. Historical spelling and source punctuation are retained; abbreviation glyphs and compound hyphens are normalized. The English title is descriptive.

The supplied introduction leaves do not show printed folios. The page range 5–6 follows the existing bibliography. Publication year 1950 follows the bibliography and תש״י on the title page; the introduction's earlier תש״ט date is preserved as source text.

Printed lines are reflowed, words divided only by line endings are joined, and no editorial page markers are added. Meaningful source spellings and citations are retained. Small-cap names/signatures use normal case. Check the entire edition and all quoted material against the scan before acceptance; transcription checking does not independently verify historical claims.
