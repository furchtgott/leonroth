## Editorial choices and review

This edition follows the retained 1972 reprint of the 1955 Jewish Book Week lecture. The source’s republication note is clearly labelled and placed after the essay. Borrow’s dialogue, Hebrew quotations, and printed emphasis are retained. Adjacent articles and book title material are excluded from the body.

The year here identifies the scanned reprint. The existing bibliography also records the 1955 London Jewish Book Council publication; it remains unchanged.

Printed lines are reflowed, words divided only by line endings are joined, and no editorial page markers are added. Meaningful source spellings and citations are retained. Small-cap names/signatures use normal case.
