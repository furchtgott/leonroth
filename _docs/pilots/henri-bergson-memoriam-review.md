## Editorial choices and review

The title’s asterisk note is attached to the end of the opening paragraph in HTML. Symmetric quotation marks are encoded as &quot; to prevent English smart-quote conversion; Hebrew abbreviation marks remain ׳ and ״. A proficient Hebrew reader should check the entire draft, especially names, the title’s vowel points, and the source-note wording.

Printed lines are reflowed, words divided only by line endings are joined, and no editorial page markers are added. Meaningful source spellings and citations are retained. Small-cap names/signatures use normal case.
