## Editorial choices and review

Seven original sections and all 23 source notes are retained. Notes originally restart on each printed page and now have continuous Markdown labels, with their attachment order unchanged. French and Latin wording and source citation numerals are retained; the journal’s article ordinal III is excluded from the body.

The source prints “has lead”, the French phrase “cette endroit”, the date 1690 for Of Education, and 1666 in its Newton note. These are retained as transcription readings, not endorsed or silently corrected. Review all French/Latin quotations and citation numerals before acceptance.

Printed lines are reflowed, words divided only by line endings are joined, and no editorial page markers are added. Meaningful source spellings and citations are retained. Small-cap names/signatures use normal case.
