## Findings to review

- **Shared pages:** only Roth's own work is transcribed. Adjacent articles and reviews are excluded. The Buber review really is one sentence; it is not truncated.
- **Source editors:** the Hampshire review retains the original bracketed notice of its delayed receipt. The Brandon review retains the journal editors' notice of Roth's death as a linked footnote at his signature. These notices are attributed to the original editors, not the archive.
- **Bibliography discrepancies:** the Brandon scan is printed on pages 271–277, rather than the bibliography's 217 f. The Aquinas title follows the scan's Thomae / Opuscula. Hampshire's issue number is left unset because the supplied scan does not confirm the bibliography's issue 1. Existing bibliography pages remain unchanged.
- **Hebrew introduction:** the text is Roth's מבוא on PDF pages 4–5, not Ben-Sasson's הקדמה on page 2. Its Elul תש״ט date is retained; publication metadata uses 1950, following the existing bibliography and the book's תש״י title page. The supplied introduction leaves have no visible folios, so 5–6 is attributed to the bibliography. A proficient Hebrew reader should review it.
- **Source quality:** A Plea for Universality in Education was deferred because text disappears into the binding. Judah Leon Magnes: An Appreciation has a blot/crease over words and was also deferred. Both are catalog entries only; neither has a reading page. Yovel Spinoza remains deferred from the earlier batch.
