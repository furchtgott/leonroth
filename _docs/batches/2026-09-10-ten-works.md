## Editorial findings

- All 75 pages in the ten original/public PDF pairs are pixel-identical at 170 dpi. Every article page was read visually, with enlarged checks of doubtful text. Text extraction and local Apple Vision OCR supplied proposals only; the three Hebrew works were transcribed directly from the images.
- Great Jewish Books follows the 1972 reprint of a 1955 lecture. The West London address follows its 2006 European Judaism reprint and retains its 1956 delivery date in the notes. Its original section-number jump from 7 to 9 remains. Reprint editorial notes are labelled separately from Roth’s prose. The existing bibliography citations remain untouched.
- The Discourse on Method includes all 23 notes, with the source-page/number correspondence recorded. Camus includes all 12 sections and its source note. Complete OCR-omitted lines were restored from scans in several English works.
- Hebrew quotes use `&quot;`; abbreviations retain geresh/gershayim. Mixed English and Latin in the Hebrew lecture use LTR `bdi` spans, and Hebrew quotations in Great Jewish Books use RTL spans. Source ornaments become horizontal rules, and the pointed Education and Religion title is retained.
- Review the difficult letterforms, scriptural citations and historical spellings in all three Hebrew editions. Education and Religion preserves a visible missing aleph in “כי השיג ת מטרתו” and irregular source quotation boundaries; its record calls these out. Apparent source errors in the West London and Discourse texts likewise remain for editorial review.
- Three additional candidates were deferred and have catalog records only: Cartesian Studies (binding loss), National Discipline and Individual Freedom (binding loss), and The Descartes–Huygens Correspondence (missing printed page 105). The catalog now has 46 editions and 11 deferred candidates.
